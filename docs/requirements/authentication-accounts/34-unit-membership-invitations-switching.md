# Requirement 34 — Unit Membership, Invitations & Switching

Status: Partly done (2026-10-23). See [ADR 0035](../../adr/0035-units-memberships-and-invitations.md).
- **Built:** the unit identity model and Council → District → Unit hierarchy with database-enforced uniqueness, 235 seeded councils, per-unit memberships (replacing ADR 0033's role on `User`), an active unit revalidated on every request, the unit switcher, email-bound single-use invitations with invite-only signup, and member removal with an audit log.
- **Blocked:**
  - Unit creation, including council lookup (REQ-34.15) and Crew/Ship/Post defaulting to Family, is not built. [Requirement 27](../multi-tenancy/27-multi-tenant-unit-isolation.md) (done 2026-10-23) removed the data-isolation blocker; who may create a unit is still open ([Requirement 38](../38-platform-admin-cross-tenant-support.md)).
  - District renames and cross-council moves wait on [Requirement 38](../38-platform-admin-cross-tenant-support.md).
  - Within-council moves wait on the adult-leader permissions pass.
  - Youth (Patrol Leader) invitations wait on [Requirement 33](../33-youth-account-creation-coppa.md). New Patrol Leader sign-ups are paused until then (user decision, 2026-10-23).
- **Deferred:** shareable invite codes. REQ-34.5 is met by email invitations alone.
- **Existing unit:** backfilled as a fictitious Troop 8 (Boys), Buckeye District, council #433, chosen by the user.
Depends on: [Requirement 31 - Core Authentication: Accounts, Sessions & Passkeys](31-core-authentication-accounts-sessions-passkeys.md). Integrates with [Requirement 33 - Adult/Guardian-Mediated Youth Account Creation (COPPA)](../33-youth-account-creation-coppa.md) whenever an invitation's recipient is a youth.

## Goal

Let a signed-in person belong to one or more Units, join a new one only through an explicit, auditable action, and switch their active unit without re-authenticating — the account-side half of multi-tenancy, built and reviewed independently of the unitId-propagation/RLS/data-isolation work in [Requirement 27](../multi-tenancy/27-multi-tenant-unit-isolation.md).

## Source

[Requirement 27](../multi-tenancy/27-multi-tenant-unit-isolation.md)'s original "Accounts, membership & switching" section (REQ-27.7 through REQ-27.10, 2026-10-18), moved here unchanged in substance as part of the 2026-10-23 restructuring that split the authentication prerequisite into independently reviewable requirements.

User direction, 2026-10-23 (follow-up): define a Unit the way Scouting America does. Unit numbers are not globally unique — Troop 332 can exist in two different districts of the same council — so uniqueness is enforced at the district level. The hierarchy is National > Council > District, and every unit must specify its Unit Type (Pack, Troop, Crew, Ship, Post), Unit Number, District, and Council. Further follow-up, same day: every council has an assigned number because council names can change — when referencing a council, either ask for the council number or look it up on the person's behalf. Further follow-up, same day: district names and a unit's district are both expected to change over time; linked troops are distinguished by a troop gender designation; and a list of councils and their numbers is seeded. Further follow-up, same day: district renames are Platform Admin-only for now (may loosen as the product matures); units must be movable to a different council, e.g. after a council merger or divestiture, also as a Platform Admin function; and Family is a third troop designation alongside Boys and Girls. Further follow-up, same day: moving a unit between districts is an adult leader function (Scoutmaster, Assistant Scoutmaster, Committee Chair, or Committee Member), not an ASPL one, with permissions to be worked out later; and the gender designation is a general field, so Packs carry the same Boys/Girls/Family options as Troops. This replaced REQ-34.1's original freeform `name` field and added REQ-34.9 through REQ-34.16.

## Requirements

### The membership data model

- **REQ-34.1** A `Unit` entity exists, identified the way Scouting America identifies a chartered unit rather than by a freeform name: id, Unit Type, Unit Number, Gender Designation, District (and through it, Council), and createdAt — all required, per REQ-34.9 through REQ-34.16 below. A Unit has no separately stored display name; "Troop 332 (Girls)" is derived from its type, number, and designation, so they can never disagree. [Requirement 27](../multi-tenancy/27-multi-tenant-unit-isolation.md) owns propagating this id onto every other business table and enforcing isolation around it; this requirement only needs `Unit` to exist, not to be fully wired through the rest of the schema.
- **REQ-34.2** A `UnitMembership` join table associates a `User` with a `Unit` and a role (`PATROL_LEADER` or `ASPL`), plus, for a Patrol Leader, which `Patrol` they lead. A single user can hold memberships in more than one unit at once, and role is scoped per-membership, not global — the same person can be the ASPL in one unit and a Patrol Leader in another.

### Unit identity & the Scouting America hierarchy

- **REQ-34.9** Unit Type is exactly one of **Pack, Troop, Crew, Ship, Post** — a fixed enumeration, not user-creatable, the same way the patrols and the six meal-framework occasions are fixed reference data today. Recording a unit's type does not by itself make ScoutBuddy's Troop-shaped features (patrols, Patrol Leader, ASPL of Camping) apply to other unit types; see Out of Scope.
- **REQ-34.10** The hierarchy is modeled as **Council → District → Unit**. National is implicit (there is exactly one, Scouting America) and is not stored. `Council` and `District` are their own entities: every District belongs to exactly one Council, and every Unit belongs to exactly one District. A Unit's Council is always derived through its District, never stored separately on the Unit, so a unit can never claim a district that belongs to a different council. District names are unique within their Council (compared case- and whitespace-insensitively, so "Pathfinder District" and "pathfinder  district" are the same district). Councils and Districts are shared, non-unit-scoped reference data per [Requirement 27](../multi-tenancy/27-multi-tenant-unit-isolation.md)'s REQ-27.2.
  - **District names are expected to change.** Councils regularly rename and realign their districts, so a District is identified by its own id, never by its name, and its name is an editable display attribute. Renaming a district changes only that name: every unit in it stays attached, with no change to its identity, memberships, or data. A rename that would collide with another district name in the same council is rejected. Because a rename changes what every unit in that district sees, it's a [Requirement 38](../38-platform-admin-cross-tenant-support.md) Platform Admin action, not something one unit's ASPL can do to the others. This is a deliberate starting point, not a permanent rule: as the product matures, rename rights may be extended (e.g. to a district-level role), and doing so is a future requirement, not a change to this one's data model.
  - **Units are expected to move between districts.** A unit's adult leadership can move their own unit to a different district in the same council. This is expected to be a Scoutmaster, Assistant Scoutmaster, Committee Chair, or Committee Member function, not an ASPL (youth) one; the exact roles and permission model are deferred to a later permissions pass, and until then nothing in this requirement grants it to an ASPL. The new district can be either an existing one or a newly entered one. The move changes only the unit's District: it's the same unit, keeping its id, memberships, and every piece of its data. The move is re-checked against REQ-34.11 (and rejected with REQ-34.13's message if the new district already has that unit) and logged with who moved it, when, and from which district to which.
  - **Units and districts are expected to move between councils.** Councils merge and divest territory, so a Platform Admin (only) can (a) move a single unit to a district in a different council, and (b) move a whole district, with every unit in it, to a different council. A council merger is (b) applied to every district of the absorbed council; a divestiture is (a) or (b) applied to the units or districts that change hands. Either way, each unit stays the same unit — same id, memberships, and data — and its Council changes only because its District's Council did, so the "council always derived through district" rule above still holds. A unit move is re-checked against REQ-34.11 in its new district; a district move is rejected if the target council already has a district with that name (the admin renames or merges first). Every move is logged with who, when, and from/to which district and council. A council left with no districts after a merger is marked inactive — kept for history and logs, no longer suggested by REQ-34.15's lookup — never deleted.
- **REQ-34.14** A Council is identified by its **Scouting America council number**, not its name. Council names change (renames, mergers), so the council number is the unique, stable key and the name is an editable display attribute — renaming a council never creates a second council or touches the units under it. Leading zeros in a council number are not significant, the same rule as REQ-34.12.
- **REQ-34.15** Whenever a person specifies a council (e.g. while setting up a unit), they either enter the council number directly or look it up by name: typing part of a council's name suggests matching councils with their numbers, drawn from councils already in ScoutBuddy plus a seeded reference list of Scouting America councils and their numbers. The person always confirms the match — a council is never assigned from a name guess without that confirmation. If the reference list has no match, the person enters the number themselves. If an entered number already exists in ScoutBuddy under a different name, the person is shown the existing name and asked to confirm it's the same council, rather than silently creating a duplicate or silently renaming it.
- **REQ-34.11** A unit is unique on **(District, Unit Type, Unit Number, Gender Designation)**, enforced by a database unique constraint, not only an application check. Gender Designation (REQ-34.16) is required on every unit, so no part of the key is ever empty. So, all within one council: Troop 332 in two different districts is allowed; Troop 332 and Pack 332 in the same district is allowed (separate units that often share a chartered organization); Troop 332 (Boys) and Troop 332 (Girls) in the same district is allowed (a linked pair), as is a Troop 332 (Family) alongside either, and the same holds for Packs; a second Troop 332 (Boys) or a second Pack 332 (Family) in the same district is rejected.
- **REQ-34.16** Every unit has a **Gender Designation** of **Boys**, **Girls**, or **Family** (Family means both boys and girls are welcome). It exists because a linked pair of single-gender Troops or Packs can share a unit number in the same district. It's a general field on `Unit`, required for every unit type: a Pack or Troop chooses it explicitly, while a Crew, Ship, or Post defaults to Family (those programs are co-ed) but can still be set like any other unit. It's part of the unit's identity (REQ-34.11) and its derived display name, e.g. "Troop 332 (Girls)" or "Pack 332 (Family)".
- **REQ-34.12** Unit Number is a positive whole number (1–9999, matching Scouting America's four-digit unit numbers). Leading zeros are not significant — "0332" and "332" are the same unit number for uniqueness — and it is displayed without padding ("Troop 332").
- **REQ-34.13** Anywhere a person chooses among units or confirms which unit they're joining — the unit switcher (REQ-34.4), invite acceptance (REQ-34.5), and [Requirement 38](../38-platform-admin-cross-tenant-support.md)'s operator tools — the unit is shown with its District and Council (e.g., with made-up names, "Troop 332 · North Star District · Lakeside Council #123"), never by type and number alone, since type and number alone are ambiguous. If creating or editing a unit collides with an existing one under REQ-34.11, the error says only that that unit is already registered in ScoutBuddy and to ask its ASPL for an invite — it reveals nothing beyond the identity the person just entered themselves (no member names, no unit data), consistent with [Requirement 27](../multi-tenancy/27-multi-tenant-unit-isolation.md)'s REQ-27.13.

### Active unit & switching

- **REQ-34.3** The signed-in person's active unit is tracked server-side (e.g. `activeUnitId` on their session) and revalidated against their actual `UnitMembership` rows on every request — never inferred from a request parameter, and never trusted from a client-supplied value alone. A tampered or stale `activeUnitId` must never grant access to a unit the person isn't a member of.
- **REQ-34.4** A unit switcher is reachable from anywhere in the app (e.g. the header, alongside or replacing today's role picker), lists only units the signed-in person actually belongs to, and switching is a single action that returns them to an equivalent page scoped to the new unit — never leaving a page still showing the previous unit's data mid-navigation.

### Joining & leaving

- **REQ-34.5** Joining a unit happens only through an explicit, auditable action: an invite (email-bound, single-use, expiring) sent by that unit's ASPL, or a shareable invite code (short-lived, capped-use) — never inferred from matching email domains, names, or any other heuristic. A person is never silently added to a unit they didn't take an action to join.
- **REQ-34.6** An invite targeting a youth follows [Requirement 33](../33-youth-account-creation-coppa.md)'s adult-mediated creation path — the inviting adult (or the youth's parent/guardian) completes account creation on the youth's behalf, rather than the youth accepting the invite directly with a personal email.
- **REQ-34.7** Invitation endpoints require email verification before an invite can be accepted. This is a named, real vulnerability class — CVE-2026-53514 affects an invitation flow in exactly this way, letting an unverified session accept an invite if it obtains the invitation ID — so whichever invitation mechanism/library is chosen at build time is checked against its own current security advisories before launch, not assumed safe by reputation.
- **REQ-34.8** Removing a person from a unit deletes (or deactivates) only their `UnitMembership` row for that unit — never their global `User` account, and never any membership they hold in a different unit. If their active session's `activeUnitId` pointed at the removed unit, it's forced to re-resolve on their next request rather than left dangling. The removal is logged (who removed whom, when).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Every Unit has a Unit Type, Unit Number, and District, and its Council is derived through that District; a unit cannot be created with any of them missing. (Enforced by NOT NULL columns; there's no council column on `Unit`.)
- [x] Tests confirm, at the database level: two Troop 332 (Boys) units in different districts of the same council can both exist; Troop 332 and Pack 332 in the same district can both exist; Troop 332 (Boys) and Troop 332 (Girls) in the same district can both exist; a second Troop 332 (Boys) (including one entered as "0332") or a second Pack 332 (Family) in the same district is rejected. (Verified on 2026-10-23 with a one-off script against the dev branch, including the 1–9999 CHECK constraint. The project has no automated database test harness yet. Treating "0332" as 332 is covered by `lib/units.test.ts`.)
- [ ] Every unit has a Boys, Girls, or Family Gender Designation; Packs and Troops must choose one, and Crews, Ships, and Posts default to Family. (The required column is built. The Family default belongs to the unit-creation form, which is blocked on Requirement 27.)
- [ ] Renaming a district leaves every unit in it attached and unchanged, and only a Platform Admin can do it. (Districts are already keyed by id. The rename action is blocked on Requirement 38.)
- [ ] A Platform Admin can move a unit to a district in another council, and move a whole district (with its units) to another council; afterwards every affected unit is the same unit with the same id, memberships, and data, shows its new council, and the move is logged. Neither move is available to an ASPL. (Blocked on Requirement 38.)
- [ ] A council emptied by a merger is marked inactive, no longer appears in council lookup, and is not deleted. (`Council.isActive` exists. The action is blocked on Requirement 38.)
- [ ] A unit's adult leadership can move their unit to another district in the same council (exact roles per the later permissions pass), an ASPL cannot, and afterwards it is the same unit with the same id, memberships, and data, and the move is logged. (Blocked on the permissions pass.)
- [x] The seeded council reference list is present in every environment (including the test database), so council lookup works without any manual setup. (The seed is part of the migration itself.)
- [x] The unit switcher and invite acceptance screen show each unit's District and Council alongside its type and number.
- [x] Councils are unique by council number; renaming a council leaves its units and districts attached, and entering an existing council number never creates a second council. (Database unique constraint on `Council.number`. A name is a plain attribute, so a rename touches nothing else.)
- [ ] A person setting up a unit can find their council by typing part of its name and confirming the suggested council number, or by entering the number directly. (Part of unit creation, not yet built.)
- [x] A person with memberships in two units can switch their active unit from a reachable control, with no page showing the previous unit's data after the switch. (Verified in the running app with a temporary second unit, at 360px and 1440px.)
- [x] No `UnitMembership` is ever created without a traceable invite or invite-code acceptance. (Every membership created by the app records its `invitationId`. The only exceptions are the one-time `MIGRATION` rows that backfilled pre-existing accounts.)
- [x] A tampered or stale `activeUnitId` is tested, not just coded, to never grant access to a unit the person isn't a member of. (`lib/session.test.ts`'s `resolveActiveMembership` cases.)
- [x] Removing a person from Unit A leaves their Unit B membership, and their account as a whole, completely intact. (Removal deletes one `(user, unit)` membership row. Verified in the running app: a removed account still exists and lands on `/no-unit`.)
- [x] The invitation mechanism in use is confirmed patched against known invitation-acceptance vulnerabilities (e.g. CVE-2026-53514-class issues) before launch. (No third-party invitation library is used. The in-house flow needs the emailed secret token, not an invitation id, and is email-bound. See ADR 0035.)

## Out of Scope for This Requirement

- Propagating `unitId` to every other business table, Row-Level Security, and the cross-tenant isolation test suite — [Requirement 27](../multi-tenancy/27-multi-tenant-unit-isolation.md).
- Export or deletion of an entire unit's data — [Requirement 27](../multi-tenancy/27-multi-tenant-unit-isolation.md)'s REQ-27.15.
- Disabling or deleting a person's account entirely (as opposed to removing one membership) — [Requirement 35](../35-user-account-disabling-deletion.md).
- Features specific to non-Troop unit types (dens and Den Leaders for a Pack, etc.). REQ-34.9 records every unit's type so the data model doesn't need a later migration, but ScoutBuddy's planning features stay Troop-shaped until a future requirement says otherwise.
- Keeping the council reference list (REQ-34.15) automatically in sync with Scouting America, or any reference list of districts. The council list is seeded once and updated by hand when needed; Districts are entered as units are set up, deduplicated per REQ-34.10.
- District-level or council-level roles (e.g. letting a district volunteer rename their own district). District renames and all cross-council moves are Platform Admin-only for now, per REQ-34.10.
